# Information Security Risk Assessment Methodology

**Organisation:** Northstar Digital Ltd (fictional)  
**Document ID:** NSD-ISMS-RSK-MET-001  
**Version:** 0.1 — Draft

## 1. Purpose
Provide a consistent, repeatable method for identifying, analysing, evaluating and treating information security risks affecting the proposed ISMS.

## 2. Risk statement format
Record risks as: **Because of [threat / cause] exploiting [vulnerability / condition], [event] may occur, resulting in [impact on confidentiality, integrity, availability or business objectives].**

## 3. Assessment scale

### Likelihood
| Score | Descriptor | Illustrative interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Exceptional; no known occurrence and strong preventive measures |
| 2 | Unlikely | Could occur, but not expected in normal circumstances |
| 3 | Possible | Credible scenario; may occur |
| 4 | Likely | Expected to occur in many circumstances |
| 5 | Almost certain | Frequent or imminent absent intervention |

### Impact
| Score | Descriptor | Illustrative interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Insignificant | Negligible operational or information impact |
| 2 | Minor | Limited disruption or low-sensitivity exposure |
| 3 | Moderate | Material team/service impact or reportable issue requiring assessment |
| 4 | Major | Significant customer, financial, legal or operational impact |
| 5 | Severe | Prolonged critical service outage, extensive sensitive-data compromise or serious business harm |

## 4. Scoring
**Risk score = Likelihood × Impact.** Score range: 1–25.

| Score | Rating | Proposed response |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low | Manage through routine controls; monitor |
| 5–9 | Moderate | Assign owner and proportionate treatment or documented acceptance |
| 10–16 | High | Prioritised treatment; management oversight |
| 17–25 | Critical | Immediate escalation and treatment decision by senior management |

These thresholds are illustrative and must be approved by the organisation.

## 5. Inherent and residual risk
- **Inherent risk:** assessed before considering controls.
- **Residual risk:** assessed after considering the design and operating effectiveness of existing controls.
- Do not reduce a score merely because a control is listed. Record evidence of implementation and effectiveness before crediting it.

## 6. Risk treatment options
- Modify / mitigate: implement or improve controls.
- Avoid: stop or change the activity creating the risk.
- Share / transfer: use contractual or insurance arrangements where appropriate; accountability remains with the organisation.
- Retain / accept: formally accept within approved risk appetite and delegated authority.

## 7. Ownership and review
Every risk must have an accountable business risk owner. The owner should agree treatment, target dates and residual-risk acceptance. Review high and critical risks at least monthly, other risks quarterly, and all relevant risks after material changes or incidents.

## 8. Limitations
This methodology is a simulated starting point. A real assessment requires workshops, evidence, asset and process owners, threat intelligence where appropriate, legal and contractual context, and management-approved risk criteria.
