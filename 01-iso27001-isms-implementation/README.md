# Project 01 — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS implementation

**Status:** In progress — Phase 1 (context, scope, assets and initial risk identification)  
**Scenario:** Fictional UK SaaS company, Northstar Digital Ltd  
**Portfolio classification:** Simulated case study; all company details and data are fictional.

## 1. Business scenario
Northstar Digital Ltd is a fictional UK-based software-as-a-service (SaaS) provider with 75 employees. It develops and operates a customer relationship and workflow platform for small and medium-sized businesses. The service is hosted in a public cloud environment. Staff work from a London office and remotely across the UK.

The company processes customer contact details, account information, support tickets and service usage data. It relies on cloud hosting, identity services, software development tools and external support suppliers.

## 2. Project objectives
- Define a defensible proposed ISMS scope.
- Identify internal and external context and interested parties.
- Establish an initial information asset inventory.
- Define a repeatable risk assessment method.
- Identify and score initial information security risks.
- Plan risk treatment and evidence collection in later phases.

## 3. Framework and approach
The exercise is informed by ISO/IEC 27001:2022, including its management-system approach and risk assessment/treatment requirements. It is not a reproduction of the standard. The organisation must determine applicable requirements and controls based on its context and risk assessment.

## 5. Important limitations
This is a simulated portfolio exercise. No real organisation has been assessed. No certification claim is made. The scope, risks, ratings and control decisions are illustrative and would require validation by actual business owners, technical teams and management before operational use.
