# Organisational Context and Interested Parties

**Organisation:** Northstar Digital Ltd (fictional)  
**Document ID:** NSD-ISMS-CTX-001  
**Version:** 0.1 — Draft

## 1. Internal and external issues

| Category | Issue | Potential ISMS relevance |
|---|---|---|
| External | UK data protection and contractual security obligations | Confidentiality, lawful processing, customer assurance |
| External | Cloud and SaaS supply-chain dependency | Availability, supplier assurance, concentration risk |
| External | Increasing phishing and credential theft | Account compromise and unauthorised access |
| External | Customer expectations for service resilience | Availability, incident response and recovery |
| Internal | 75-person organisation with limited specialist capacity | Clear ownership, proportionate controls and awareness |
| Internal | Remote and hybrid working | Endpoint security, identity controls and physical security |
| Internal | Frequent software releases | Secure development, change control and vulnerability management |
| Internal | Reliance on cloud-hosted production services | Configuration, access, backup and resilience management |

## 2. Interested parties and relevant needs

| Interested party | Relevant needs / expectations | How the ISMS may address them |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | Confidentiality, availability, incident communication | Contractual controls, access management, incident process |
| Employees and contractors | Clear policies, usable controls, training | Awareness, acceptable-use rules, role-based access |
| Regulators | Compliance with applicable legal obligations | Legal register, privacy governance, evidence and accountability |
| Senior management | Visibility of material risks and performance | Risk reporting, objectives and management review |
| Cloud providers | Defined shared responsibilities and secure configuration | Supplier due diligence and service reviews |
| Investors / owners | Protection of business assets and continuity | Risk treatment, resilience and governance |
| Business partners | Secure information exchange | Agreements, access restrictions and secure transfer |

## 3. ISMS-relevant processes
- Governance and risk management.
- Product design and software development.
- Cloud service operations.
- Identity and endpoint administration.
- Customer onboarding and support.
- Supplier selection and oversight.
- Incident response and business continuity.

## 4. Climate change consideration
As part of the 2024 amendment to ISO management-system standards, the organisation should determine whether climate change is a relevant issue in its context and whether interested parties have relevant requirements. For this fictional SaaS provider, potential considerations include climate-related disruption to data-centre regions, office access and supplier continuity. The relevance and any resulting actions would need to be evaluated and recorded by management.

## 5. Review
Review this analysis annually and after significant changes to the organisation, technology, market, legal obligations, suppliers or stakeholder expectations.
