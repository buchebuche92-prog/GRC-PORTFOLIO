## 1. Purpose
This document defines the proposed boundaries and applicability of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) for the fictional organisation Northstar Digital Ltd. It is a portfolio exercise and must be validated before use in a real organisation.

## 2. Organisation overview
Northstar Digital Ltd is a fictional UK SaaS provider with approximately 75 employees. It designs, develops, hosts and supports a cloud-based customer relationship and workflow platform for business customers.

## 3. Proposed scope
The proposed ISMS covers the people, processes, information, technology and facilities used to design, develop, operate, maintain and support the Northstar SaaS platform, including:
- Product engineering and software development lifecycle activities.
- Production cloud infrastructure and supporting cloud services.
- Corporate identity, endpoint and collaboration services used by in-scope personnel.
- Customer onboarding, service support and platform operations.
- Information security governance, risk management, supplier oversight and incident management.
- The London office and remote-working arrangements insofar as they support in-scope activities.

**Proposed scope statement:**  
"The Information Security Management System of Northstar Digital Ltd applies to the design, development, operation and support of its cloud-based customer relationship and workflow platform, including the supporting people, processes, information, technology, facilities and suppliers operating from the United Kingdom."

## 4. Interfaces and dependencies
The ISMS has interfaces with:
- Public cloud hosting provider.
- Identity and access management provider.
- Source-code hosting and CI/CD providers.
- Customer support and CRM platforms.
- Managed IT and security service providers.
- Customers and business partners whose information is processed.

Supplier services are not automatically within Northstar's organisational boundary; however, risks arising from their services and the interfaces with them must be assessed and managed.

## 5. Exclusions and boundary decisions
No ISO/IEC 27001 management-system requirements are excluded by this scope statement. Specific Annex A control applicability decisions are to be documented separately in the Statement of Applicability, with justifications.

The scope does not claim that supplier organisations themselves are certified or included within Northstar's ISMS. Their relevant services and risks are addressed through supplier governance.

## 6. Assumptions requiring validation
- The organisation has one UK legal entity.
- The platform is hosted in a public cloud environment.
- Customer data is processed to provide the SaaS service.
- No development or production operations are outsourced in full.
- The London office is the only permanent office.
- Remote workers access corporate systems using managed endpoints.

## 7. Review and approval
Review at least annually and when there are material changes to services, legal entities, locations, technology, suppliers or business processes.
