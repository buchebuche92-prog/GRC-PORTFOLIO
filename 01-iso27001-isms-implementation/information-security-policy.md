## 1. Purpose

Northstar Digital Ltd recognises that information is a critical business asset and that protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information is essential to maintaining customer trust, business continuity and regulatory compliance.

The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to establish the organisation's overall direction, principles and commitments for managing information security.

This policy provides the foundation for the establishment, implementation, maintenance and continual improvement of the organisation's Information Security Management System (ISMS), aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

It establishes the information security expectations that employees, contractors, management and relevant third parties are required to understand and follow.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, directors, contractors and temporary personnel.
- All information assets owned, processed, stored or managed by Northstar Digital Ltd.
- The organisation's customer-facing SaaS platform and supporting infrastructure.
- Corporate systems, networks, applications, databases and cloud environments.
- Third-party suppliers and service providers that access or process organisational information.

The ISMS boundaries and applicability are defined in the organisation's documented ISMS Scope Statement.

This policy applies to all activities, personnel, information assets and supporting technologies falling within the defined ISMS scope.

## 3. Information Security Objectives

Northstar Digital Ltd is committed to protecting information through the following objectives:

1. **Confidentiality:** Ensure information is accessible only to authorised individuals and systems.
2. **Integrity:** Protect information against unauthorised alteration, destruction or corruption.
3. **Availability:** Ensure information and services remain accessible to authorised users when required.
4. **Risk management:** Identify, assess and treat information security risks using an established risk management methodology.
5. **Legal and regulatory compliance:** Identify and meet applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and customer security requirements.
6. **Incident management:** Establish processes to identify, report, investigate and respond to information security incidents.
7. **Continual improvement:** Regularly evaluate the effectiveness of the ISMS and improve its performance.

The organisation shall maintain measurable information security objectives, including relevant targets, responsible owners, monitoring arrangements and review intervals.

These objectives shall be established and reviewed by management and aligned with the organisation's business priorities and information security risks.

## 4. Management Commitment

Senior management shall demonstrate leadership and commitment to information security by:

- Establishing, approving and supporting the organisation's Information Security Policy and objectives.
- Ensuring information security requirements are integrated into relevant business processes.
- Providing appropriate resources, personnel and technology to support the ISMS.
- Assigning and communicating information security roles and responsibilities.
- Promoting awareness of information security risks and individual obligations.
- Supporting the information security risk assessment and risk treatment processes.
- Reviewing ISMS performance and supporting continual improvement.
- Ensuring that information security responsibilities are understood throughout the organisation.

Senior management explicitly commits to:

- Establishing a framework for setting and reviewing measurable information security objectives.
- Satisfying applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and information security requirements.
- Continually improving the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the ISMS.
- Ensuring this policy is communicated, understood and applied within the organisation.

Information security is a shared responsibility and shall be considered in business planning, operational decision-making and supplier management.

## 5. Information Security Principles

Northstar Digital Ltd shall apply the following principles.

### 5.1 Access Control

Access to information and systems shall be restricted according to business requirements and the principle of least privilege.

Access shall be authorised, periodically reviewed and removed promptly when no longer required.

Appropriate authentication mechanisms shall be implemented to protect access to organisational systems and information.

### 5.2 Asset Management

Information assets shall be identified, recorded, assigned appropriate ownership and classified according to their sensitivity and business value.

Asset owners shall ensure that appropriate protection measures are implemented and that asset records are maintained.

### 5.3 Acceptable Use

Employees and authorised users shall use organisational information, devices, systems and services responsibly and only for legitimate business purposes.

Unauthorised access, disclosure, copying, modification or misuse of information is prohibited.

### 5.4 Data Protection and Privacy

Personal data shall be collected, processed, stored, shared and retained in accordance with applicable data protection requirements, including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, where applicable.

Appropriate technical and organisational measures shall be implemented to protect personal data.

Personal data shall only be accessed and processed for legitimate and authorised purposes.

### 5.5 Cloud and Infrastructure Security

Cloud environments and supporting infrastructure shall be configured, monitored and maintained using appropriate security controls.

Responsibilities between Northstar Digital Ltd and its cloud service providers shall be understood and documented.

Security responsibilities shall be considered throughout the acquisition, configuration, operation and decommissioning of cloud services.

### 5.6 Incident Management

All suspected or actual information security incidents shall be reported through the organisation's designated reporting process.

Incidents shall be assessed, recorded, investigated and addressed according to their severity and potential business impact.

Lessons learned from incidents shall be considered when improving information security controls and processes.

### 5.7 Supplier Security

Third-party suppliers that process, store or access organisational information shall be assessed according to the security risks they introduce.

Relevant security requirements shall be incorporated into supplier agreements and monitored throughout the supplier relationship.

### 5.8 Business Continuity

Appropriate arrangements shall be maintained to support the availability and recovery of critical information systems and services following disruption.

Recovery arrangements shall be periodically reviewed and tested according to business requirements and identified risks.

## 6. Information Security Risk Management

Northstar Digital Ltd shall maintain a documented information security risk assessment and treatment process.

The process shall:

- Identify information security risks affecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information.
- Assess risks using approved likelihood and impact criteria.
- Determine risk levels and prioritise treatment activities.
- Identify appropriate risk treatment options and controls.
- Assign risk owners and treatment responsibilities.
- Record risk acceptance decisions and obtain appropriate approval.
- Review risks periodically and following significant organisational, technological or security changes, including incidents.

The organisation shall maintain a documented risk register and risk treatment plan as part of its ISMS.

Risk treatment decisions shall be proportionate to the level of risk and aligned with organisational objectives and the organisation's defined risk acceptance criteria.

Residual risks shall be reviewed and formally accepted by authorised risk owners where appropriate.

## 7. Roles and Responsibilities

### 7.1 Senior Management

Senior management shall:

- Approve the Information Security Policy.
- Provide resources for the ISMS.
- Establish and communicate information security responsibilities.
- Review ISMS performance.
- Support appropriate risk treatment and continual improvement.
- Ensure that significant information security risks are escalated appropriately.

### 7.2 Information Security Manager

The Information Security Manager shall:

- Coordinate the implementation and maintenance of the ISMS.
- Support information security risk assessments.
- Monitor compliance with information security requirements.
- Coordinate policy reviews and security awareness activities.
- Report significant information security issues to senior management.
- Coordinate the monitoring and improvement of security controls.
- Maintain relevant ISMS documentation and records.

### 7.3 Asset Owners

Asset owners shall:

- Maintain an understanding of the information assets under their responsibility.
- Ensure appropriate classification and protection.
- Review access permissions where required.
- Identify and report risks affecting their assets.
- Support the implementation of appropriate risk treatment measures.

### 7.4 Employees and Contractors

All employees and contractors shall:

- Comply with this policy and supporting security procedures.
- Protect information entrusted to them.
- Complete assigned security awareness training.
- Use approved systems and technologies.
- Report suspected security incidents promptly.
- Protect authentication credentials and access devices.
- Follow relevant data protection and acceptable-use requirements.

### 7.5 Third-Party Suppliers

Suppliers shall comply with applicable contractual security requirements and agreed information protection obligations.

Where required, suppliers shall provide information or evidence to support the organisation's supplier security assurance activities.

## 8. Legal, Regulatory and Contractual Requirements

Northstar Digital Ltd shall identify, document and periodically review applicable information security, privacy and contractual requirements.

These may include:

- UK GDPR.
- Data Protection Act 2018.
- Relevant contractual and customer security obligations.
- Applicable regulatory requirements.
- Relevant intellectual property and confidentiality obligations.

A compliance obligations register shall be maintained to support monitoring and demonstrate how applicable requirements are addressed.

The organisation shall consider changes in applicable requirements and assess their potential impact on the ISMS.

## 9. Security Awareness and Training

The organisation shall provide appropriate information security awareness and training to employees and relevant contractors.

Training shall address relevant topics such as:

- Phishing and social engineering.
- Password and authentication security.
- Handling and classification of information.
- Data protection and privacy.
- Secure remote working.
- Incident identification and reporting.
- Responsibilities under organisational security policies.

Training effectiveness shall be evaluated periodically, and records of relevant training activities shall be maintained.

## 10. Monitoring, Review and Continual Improvement

The organisation shall monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of its ISMS through appropriate activities, including:

- Information security performance monitoring.
- Internal audits.
- Risk assessments and risk reviews.
- Security incident analysis.
- Management reviews.
- Corrective actions and improvement activities.

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and whenever significant organisational, technological, legal or security changes occur.

Updates shall be documented and submitted to the appropriate approval authority.

### 10.1 Policy Communication and Availability

This policy shall be communicated to all relevant employees, contractors and other applicable internal stakeholders.

The organisation shall ensure that relevant personnel understand their information security responsibilities and obligations under this policy.

The policy shall be made available to relevant interested parties where appropriate, subject to management approval, contractual obligations and information classification requirements.

The organisation shall maintain appropriate records of policy approval, communication and review.

## 11. Policy Exceptions

Any exception to this policy shall be formally documented, justified and assessed for information security risk.

Exceptions must be approved by an authorised management representative, with appropriate risk treatment measures and an agreed review or expiry date.

Approved exceptions shall be recorded and periodically reviewed to ensure that they remain justified and that associated risks are managed appropriately.

## 12. Policy Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in investigation and appropriate corrective or disciplinary action in accordance with organisational procedures and applicable contractual or legal obligations.

Where a supplier or contractor breaches applicable security requirements, the organisation shall take appropriate action in accordance with the relevant agreement.

Significant or repeated non-compliance shall be escalated to the appropriate management authority.

## 13. Approval and Document Control

This policy shall become effective following formal approval by senior management.

The document owner shall ensure that the policy is maintained, reviewed and updated as necessary.

Only the current approved version shall be treated as the authoritative organisational policy.

Superseded versions shall be retained in accordance with the organisation's document retention and control requirements.
