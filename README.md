## Portfolio Approach

Each project uses a fictional organisation and synthetic data to demonstrate how GRC concepts can be applied in realistic business scenarios.

The exercises are designed to demonstrate practical understanding, analytical thinking, risk-based decision-making and structured documentation.

All materials are learning exercises. They do not represent client engagements, independent audit opinions, legal advice or certification claims.
